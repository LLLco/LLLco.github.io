(Not so) little fact about me, TES III: Morrowind is one of my favorite games ever made. As such, it only felt natural once I got into game design to join one of the game dev teams I looked up to the most: Skywind!

I joined the team around July 2025, contributing what work I can as I finish up my studies at University.
To date, I have contributed about 400 hours to 4 cells, mostly to my main focus, the dungeon Assarnatamat Shrine.

Far and above my favorite aspect of the project is how many cool passionate people are apart of it. Everyone is just some of the most talented people I have ever met, and I am proud that they count me among them.

ASSARNATAMAT SHRINE
Editor view of the dungeon. The entrance is circled in red.
The original dungeon can be seen on the top right.
Assarnatamat is a dungeon that can be near the focal volcano of Vvardenfell, Red Mountain. The location is along the primary pathway to the final town of the game, Ghostgate, so I felt a sense of weight designing this dungeon- lots of players may choose to visit!

It also features a new quest, as part of the restored content initiative, so I had to make sure to account for that. The theme of the dungeon and quest revolves around the chaotic nature of Mehrunes Dagon's symbol of revolution, and I decided an overwhelmingly "seismic" aesthetic would fit the job perfectly! What better symbols change than a cataclysm?

GNISIS HUTS
Dinadad Hut, my personal favorite. The resident here is an old farmer,
but she keeps it as tidy as she is able.
While Gnisis itself is a bit of a backwater town, it maintains a strong Imperial presence, as well as a decent farmer economy. It is also the site of an important artefact of Vivec, the Ash Mask.

These huts are overwhelmingly unimportant. They exist purely to give the player the feeling that the world is alive. All doors must be enterable in Elder Scrolls! Thus a careful balance had to be struck- make it interesting, make it alive, but the player should not feel like they NEED to spend time there.

Gnisis is personally one of my favorite towns, so I tried to claim as many cells as I could. More to come!

FORT DAEUS
Editor view of Fort Daeus, my first SCKE dungeon.
Starts in the sewers, exits to Riften at the top.
Fort Daeus was the test dungeon I created as part of my application onto Skywind. What I didn't realize at the time is they were not expecting me to navmesh, encounter plan, create lore, and an entire in-world dungeon of decent length with stealth routes.

Oops! I had a lot of fun making this dungeon.

Fort Daeus is a secret forgotten outpost being used by the Imperials in their plans to take over Riften. It is only connected via hidden entrance by the sewers in the Ratway, being used as a clever disguise to the spycraft within. The exit takes you outside the city walls of Riften, allowing agents to easily come and go.
