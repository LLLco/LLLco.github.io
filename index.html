PROJECTS
SKYWIND
LEVEL DESIGNER
TESR: Skywind is a full community remaster of one of my favorite games ever made, The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind. It has been an absolute pleasure to work on!

Skywind has been in development since 2016, and is currently still in active progress.
TOWER OF GENN
GAME DIRECTOR
Tower of Genn is a puzzle-strategy building game in which the player must stack various shapes in order to grow their tower.

The game has been in development for nearly a year, outside of full-time university course-work, with a team of all students. Being the director of such a spirited team has been one of my favorite experiences to date!
TRIBOX
GAMEPLAY DESIGNER
Tribox is a First-Person Online multiplayer sandbox game, that allows any number of players to connect, play with and summon various objects, fight, and interact in hand crafted maps, inspired by the likes of Half-Life Deathmatch and Garry’s mod.

Developed in Godot with a team of 8, first released in Q1 2026.
GROG
SOLE DEVELOPER
GROG is a personal solo project developed within one week, built to highlight my personal artistic capabilities within the elements of visual design.

Both primarily a visual and narrative experience, the game is a walking simulator in order to properly deliver the compelling message that lies within.
BREAKER
LEVEL DESIGNER
BREAKER is a speedrunning first person shooter, focused on delivering high octane action packed levels fit for only masters of combat. It utilizes a harsh cyberpunk art style, a brutal and emotional narrative, accompanied by improvised jazz.

Developed in Unreal Engine 5.6 by a team of 10-20, BREAKER's demo released in Q2 2026.
