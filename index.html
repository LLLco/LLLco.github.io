SKYWIND

LEVEL DESIGNER

TESR: Skywind is a full remaster of one of my favorite games ever made, The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind. It has been an absolute pleasure to work on!

As a level designer, I am responsible for reimagining dungeons and interiors from the original game to be engaging, memorable, rewarding, and downright epic.

You can currently find me bringing life to Daedric ruins and citizens' huts across Vvardenfell!

TOWER OF GENN

GAME DIRECTOR

Tower of Genn is my first large scale and currently primary project directed by me with a dynamic team of 25+ people.

As the primary designer, I designed the rules, interactions, and content that create the identity of this puzzle-strategy building game, focusing on long term strategy in a calm and encouraging environment.

The game has been in development for nearly a year, outside of full-time university course-work, with a team of all students. Being the director of such a spirited team has been one of my favorite experiences too date!

TRIBOX

LEAD DESIGNER/PROGRAMMER

Tribox is a First-Person Online multiplayer sandbox game, that allows any number of players to connect, play with and summon various objects, fight, and interact in hand crafted maps.

Inspired by the likes of Half-Life Deathmatch and Garry’s mod, the goal with Tribox was to make an infinitely scalable multiplayer platform to create a deliver fun, interactive, and small multiplayer experiences.

Developed with a student team of 8, Tribox continues to be added to when ever time is found!
