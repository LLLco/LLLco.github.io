PROJECTS
SKYWIND
LEVEL DESIGNER

TESR: Skywind is a full remaster of one of my favorite games ever made, The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind. It has been an absolute pleasure to work on!

As a level designer, I am responsible for reimagining dungeons and interiors from the original game to be engaging, memorable, rewarding, and downright epic.

You can currently find me bringing life to Daedric ruins and citizens' huts across Vvardenfell!

TOWER OF GENN
GAME DIRECTOR
Tower of Genn is my first large scale and currently primary project directed by me with a dynamic team of 25+ people.

As the primary designer, I designed the rules, interactions, content, and play-space that create the identity of this puzzle-strategy building game, focusing on long term strategy in a calm and encouraging environment.

The game has been in development for nearly a year, outside of full-time university course-work, with a team of all students. Being the director of such a spirited team has been one of my favorite experiences to date!

TRIBOX
GAMEPLAY DESIGNER
Tribox is a First-Person Online multiplayer sandbox game, that allows any number of players to connect, play with and summon various objects, fight, and interact in hand crafted maps.

Inspired by the likes of Half-Life Deathmatch and Garry’s mod, the goal with Tribox was to make an infinitely scalable multiplayer platform to create a deliver fun, interactive, and small multiplayer experiences.

Developed with a student team of 8, Tribox continues to be added to when ever time is found!
GROG
SOLE DEVELOPER
GROG is a personal solo project developed within one week, built to highlight my personal artistic capabilities within the elements of visual design.

Both primarily a visual and narrative experience, the game is a walking simulator in order to properly deliver the compelling message that lies within.

GROG uses the basic acts of physical movement and sight to deliver the experience of “be a different person,” in the deepest sense possible- their core identity.
BREAKER
LEVEL DESIGNER
BREAKER is a speedrunning first person shooter, focused on delivering high octane action packed levels fit for only masters of combat.

BREAKER utilizes a harsh cyberpunk art style, a brutal and emotional narrative, accompanied by improvised jazz, to deliver one experience: psychotic action.

Developed in Unreal Engine 5.6 by a team of 10-20, BREAKER's demo released in Q2 2026.
