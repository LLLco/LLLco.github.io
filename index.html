Team Size: Hundreds
Time on Project: 13 months

TOWER OF GENN
GAME DIRECTOR
Tower of Genn is a puzzle-strategy building game in which the player must stack various shapes in order to grow their tower.

Team Size: 20+
Time on Project: 12 months
Contributions:
  • Total design and creative direction
  • A LOT of documents
Release(s): Nov 2026

The game has been in development outside of full-time university course-work, by a team of mostly students. Being the director of such a spirited team has been one of my favorite experiences to date!

BREAKER
LEVEL DESIGNER
BREAKER is a speedrunning first person shooter, focused on delivering high octane action packed levels fit for only masters of combat. It utilizes a harsh cyberpunk art style, a brutal and emotional narrative, accompanied by improvised jazz.

Developed in Unreal Engine 5.6 by a team of 10-20, BREAKER's demo released in Q2 2026.
